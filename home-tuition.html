Home Tuition FAQs

Is home tuition available for every subject?

Subject availability depends on the current faculty network and the learner’s requirement. Please enquire with the exact subject and class.

Can more than one student study together?

A small-group arrangement may be considered where the learners are compatible in class level, subject and schedule.

Are timings fixed?

Timings are discussed according to the student’s requirement and tutor availability.

Does Ascend provide accommodation?

No. Ascend Institute does not provide accommodation.
