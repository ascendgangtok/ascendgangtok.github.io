About Ascend Coaching Institute

Ascend Coaching Institute, Gangtok provides focused academic support through careful teaching, regular practice and individual attention. Our aim is to provide quality coaching in a disciplined and supportive learning environment.

About the Winter Crash Course

This two-month course is for students of Classes 10 and 12 who are preparing for their board examinations. Depending on each student’s preparation, it provides syllabus teaching, revision, question-paper practice, tests and doubt clearing.

Courses offered

  • CBSE Class 10Two programmes: Complete Syllabus Support and Exam-Focused Board Practice. Regular subjects: Mathematics, Science, Social Science (SST) and English. Nepali is a separate optional subject.
  • ICSE Class 10Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Biology, with Computer as a separate subject.
  • CBSE Class 12 SciencePhysics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology.
  • CBSE Class 12 Humanities / ArtsHistory, Economics, Political Science, Geography and Sociology.

Course highlights

  • Small batches and individual academic attention
  • Syllabus teaching or revision, depending on the programme
  • Board-pattern questions and paper-solving practice
  • Regular tests and mock examinations
  • Printed question and practice papers
  • Revision and doubt clearing

Courses for CBSE Class 10

There are two separate programmes. The right one depends on how much of the syllabus the student has already studied.

REGULAR SUBJECTS · BOTH PROGRAMMES

Mathematics · Science · Social Science (SST) · English

The package fees for both programmes cover these four subjects. Nepali is not part of these packages; it is an optional subject with a separate fee (see below).

PROGRAMME A

Complete Syllabus Support

For students who have not completed their school syllabus, or who need proper teaching of important topics before the board examination.

The programme covers

  • Detailed, chapter-wise teaching of the selected subjects
  • Clear explanation of important concepts
  • Regular practice of questions and exercises
  • Revision of topics already completed
  • Board-pattern question practice
  • Tests and doubt clearing

Packages of 2, 3 or 4 regular subjects.
Minimum enrolment: two regular subjects.

PROGRAMME B

Exam-Focused Board Practice

For students who have completed, or almost completed, their syllabus and need focused preparation for the board examination.

The programme covers

  • Focused revision of each subject
  • Board-pattern question solving
  • Previous years’ question-paper practice
  • Additional question-solving sessions
  • Timed tests and mock examinations
  • Answer-writing practice
  • Identifying and correcting common mistakes
  • Individual doubt clearing

Packages of 1, 2 or 3 regular subjects (maximum three).

CBSE CLASS 10 · SEPARATE FEE

Nepali — Optional Subject

Nepali is not included in the regular package fees. It is an optional subject with a separate fee of ₹4,000 for the complete two-month course.

Nepali can be taken only together with a package of at least two regular subjects, in either programme. It cannot be taken on its own.

Nepali classes will be held only if at least six students enrol for Nepali. If fewer than six students enrol, Nepali classes will not be held, and the full amount paid for Nepali tuition will be refunded.

This refund covers the Nepali fee only. It does not apply to fees paid for any other subject package.

Admission and Course Information

Registration and seat booking

  • Registration feeSeparate from the course fee; not adjusted against it₹500
  • Seat-booking advanceAdjusted against the selected package fee₹2,000

Both amounts (₹2,500 in total) can be paid online when registering on our website. A seat is confirmed after the institute has verified the payment.

Course commencement

Some batches begin from 10 December 2026.
All remaining batches begin by 20 December 2026.

Batch size

Each batch has a maximum of 15 students. If a batch is full, a separate batch may be formed.

Course fee payment

Payment of the full course fee in advance is preferred. If paying the full amount at once is difficult, the course fee may be paid in two parts:

60%at admission
40%during the first week of January 2027

The 60% paid at admission includes the ₹2,000 seat-booking advance.

Support for meritorious BPL students

Meritorious students from BPL families may be considered for a course-fee concession of up to 100%, subject to verification and approval by Ascend Coaching Institute.
