Winter Crash Course questions

What is the Winter Crash Course 2026–27?

A two-month board exam preparation course for Classes 10 and 12, held at Ascend Institute in Daragoan, Tadong, Gangtok. Teaching is in small batches, with syllabus completion or revision as each student needs, regular tests and mock examinations.

Which classes and boards are covered?

CBSE Class 10, ICSE Class 10 and CBSE Class 12. Class 12 has separate Science and Humanities (Arts) options.

When do the batches begin?

Some batches begin from 10 December 2026. All remaining batches begin by 20 December 2026.

Is the course suitable if the syllabus is not complete?

Yes. The course includes syllabus completion or revision, as required. For CBSE Class 10, the Complete Syllabus Support programme is meant for students whose syllabus is incomplete or who need detailed teaching.

Is there a separate course only for revision and exam practice?

Yes, for CBSE Class 10. Exam-Focused Board Practice is for students who have completed or nearly completed their syllabus. It concentrates on revision, board question papers, mock tests and examinations, with additional paper-solving sessions and printed practice papers.

Are Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology available?

Yes, for ICSE Class 10 and CBSE Class 12 Science, with a minimum of two subjects. ICSE Class 10 students can also take Computer, and CBSE Class 12 Humanities covers History, Economics, Political Science and Geography. For CBSE Class 10, please ask us about the subjects in each programme.

How many students are in each batch?

A maximum of 15 students.

How can parents enquire about the course?

Call or WhatsApp 7797898999, or visit us at Tejpal Pradhan Building, near the Indian Post Office, Daragoan, Tadong, Gangtok. The registration fee is ₹500, separate from the course fee. The seat-booking advance is ₹2,000, adjusted against the selected package. You can also apply online and book a seat.
